The Atelier Origin

Born in Mayfair & Mumbai

Gulabi Glow was founded to reconcile haute-couture high-fashion lip glaze with authentic South Asian botanical radiance. For generations, traditional ceremonies celebrated glowing skin and petal-soft lips with rosewater, saffron, and natural oils.

Our founders partnered with master cosmetic chemists to encapsulate these sacred botanicals inside a crystal glass formulation that refracts light with liquid gemstone brilliance.
